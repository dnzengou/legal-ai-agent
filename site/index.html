How it works

  1. POST your contract

    Send raw text to /review or a base64 PDF to /review-pdf. Up to 500k chars or 32MB.

  2. Claude reviews it

    A senior-attorney prompt + adaptive thinking + structured outputs. No hallucinated clauses — excerpts are verbatim.

  3. You get cited JSON

    Clauses, risks ranked by severity, recommendations, and character offsets anchoring each excerpt to your source.

One endpoint, structured output

# Send a contract
curl -X POST https://api.legal-ai-agent.fly.dev/review \
  -H "X-API-Key: $YOUR_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"contract_text": "This Agreement is entered into..."}'

# Response
{
  "summary": "Two-year SaaS subscription with auto-renewal...",
  "parties": ["Acme Corp", "Buyer LLC"],
  "key_clauses": [{
    "type": "auto_renewal",
    "text_excerpt": "This Agreement shall automatically renew...",
    "char_start": 1842,
    "char_end": 1907
  }],
  "risks": [{ "severity": "high", "category": "auto_renewal", ... }]
}

FAQ

Is this legal advice?

No. This is a software tool that produces a structured first-pass review. Final judgment belongs to a qualified attorney. Output may be wrong; verify before acting.

What model powers it?

Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 with adaptive thinking and high reasoning effort, called through structured outputs so responses validate against a strict schema.

Do you train on my contracts?

No. We don't store contracts, and Anthropic's API doesn't train on inputs by default. If you're on the managed tier, your data stays your data.

What jurisdictions does it understand?

You can pass a jurisdiction hint (e.g. "US-DE" or "EU") to tailor risk analysis. The underlying model has broad coverage of US and EU commercial law; specialized regions are best treated as advisory.

What about accessibility?

This site targets WCAG 2.2 AA: semantic HTML, 4.5:1 minimum contrast, keyboard-only navigation, prefers-reduced-motion support, forced-colors mode, and visible focus rings. Report any gaps — they're treated as bugs.
