        How it works
        1. You paste a contract or appeal (text or PDF) — up to 500k chars.
        2. Claude Opus 4.8 runs a senior-attorney prompt with adaptive thinking and structured outputs.
        3. The server anchors every excerpt to your source and computes the safety score deterministically from the risks — the model can't inflate it.

        Frequently asked

        How does it prevent hallucination?

        Every risk and compliance flag must attach a verbatim quote from your source. The server anchors each quote by exact substring match. Unanchored quotes force the finding to low confidence and drag down the overall score. A fabricated citation cannot score well because it cannot anchor.

        Is my document stored anywhere?

        No. Contracts are processed in memory and discarded after the response. No database, no persistence.

        Does it replace a lawyer?

        No. First-pass triage only. Judgment, negotiation strategy, and jurisdiction-specific case law still belong with a licensed attorney.

        What languages does it support?

        Anything Claude Opus 4.8 reads fluently — English, Swedish, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, and more.
